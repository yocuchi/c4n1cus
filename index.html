C4n1cus

C4n1cus is a private statistics dashboard for the YouTube channel C4n1CUS (marble-race football videos).

Purpose of the app

C4n1cus lets the channel owner see the performance of their own YouTube channel in one place: subscribers, views, watch time and per-video statistics. It connects to the owner's Google account with read-only access to YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. It does not upload, edit or delete anything, and it is not offered to other users.

Propósito de la app (ES)

C4n1cus es un panel privado de estadísticas del canal de YouTube C4n1CUS. Muestra al propietario del canal sus suscriptores, visualizaciones, tiempo de visualización y estadísticas por vídeo, usando acceso de solo lectura a YouTube Data API y YouTube Analytics API.
