Política de privacidad

Vigente desde el 14 de octubre de 2026

"C4n1CUS Stats" es un panel privado de estadísticas usado únicamente por el propietario del canal de YouTube C4n1CUS.

  • Lee datos y analíticas del canal mediante permisos de Google OAuth de solo lectura (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Los datos solo se muestran al propietario y se guardan localmente en su propio servidor.
  • Nunca se venden ni se comparten con terceros.
  • El uso de la información recibida de las API de Google cumple la Política de datos de usuario de los servicios de API de Google, incluidos los requisitos de Uso limitado.
  • Puedes revocar el acceso en https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contacto: yocuchi@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

"C4n1CUS Stats" is a private statistics dashboard used only by the owner of the C4n1CUS YouTube channel.

  • It reads YouTube channel data and analytics through read-only Google OAuth scopes (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Data is displayed only to the owner and stored locally on the owner's server.
  • Data is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: yocuchi@gmail.com

C4n1CUS

Data accessed and how it is used

  • Google account scopes: youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly (read-only).
  • Data read: channel name and ID, subscriber, view and video counts, list of the channel's videos with titles, publish dates and statistics, and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, traffic sources) for the owner's own channel.
  • Use: solely to display these statistics to the channel owner in the C4n1cus dashboard.
  • Storage: the OAuth token and a cached copy of the statistics are stored on the owner's private server, accessible only through the owner's private network. Cached data is overwritten on every refresh; nothing else is retained.
  • Sharing: data is never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.
  • Deletion: revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; on request to yocuchi@gmail.com all stored tokens and cached data are deleted.
  • C4n1cus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
